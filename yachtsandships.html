Discover Luxury Yacht Experiences in the Galapagos

Embark on a journey like no other with our luxury yacht experiences in the Galapagos Islands. Revel in the elegance and comfort of our meticulously designed yachts as you explore the pristine waters and untouched landscapes of this unique archipelago. Our luxury yachts offer unparalleled access to off-the-beaten-path destinations such as Fernandina, Genovesa, and West Isabela. These secluded sites promise intimate encounters with diverse wildlife and breathtaking scenery, far from the typical tourist trails.


Seaman Journey and Sea Star: Pinnacles of Luxury and Adventure.

Our two flagship yachts, the Seaman Journey and the Sea Star, represent the epitome of luxury and adventure in the Galapagos.


Seaman Journey

The Seaman Journey is a first-class motor catamaran that combines speed, comfort, and stability. With spacious cabins, elegant interiors, and a dedicated crew, guests can enjoy the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. The yacht is equipped with modern amenities, including sun decks, a hot tub, and gourmet dining, ensuring every moment aboard is memorable.



Sea Star

The Sea Star, a luxury motor yacht, offers an intimate and exclusive experience with its refined design and superior service. Accommodating a limited number of guests, the Sea Star provides spacious suites with private balconies, a large solarium, and Jacuzzis. Guests can indulge in exquisite cuisine and personalized service, making it the ideal choice for those seeking both luxury and adventure.



Explore Premier Galapagos Sites

Our luxury yachts cover some of the most significant and captivating sites in the Galapagos, including:

Gordon Rocks: Renowned for its strong currents and thrilling drift dives, where hammerhead sharks and manta rays are often seen.

North Seymour: Famous for its colonies of blue-footed boobies, frigatebirds, and playful sea lions.

Cousins Rocks: A haven for divers, offering encounters with sea turtles, seahorses, and vibrant coral formations.

Mosquera: Ideal for spotting garden eels, stingrays, and occasional sharks, perfect for both novice and experienced divers.

Daphne Menor: A tranquil spot known for its diverse fish species and volcanic formations.

Bartholomew: Home to Pinnacle Rock, where you can see Galapagos penguins, sea turtles, and white-tip reef sharks.

Floreana: Rich in history and marine life, Floreana offers a unique blend of underwater and terrestrial wonders.
