Pre and Post-Tours on Mainland Ecuador

Enhance your Galapagos adventure with our exclusive pre and post-tours on mainland Ecuador. We take pride in offering immersive experiences in some of the country's most iconic destinations:


Quito: Explore the historic capital with its rich colonial architecture and vibrant culture. Otavalo: Visit the famous indigenous market and witness traditional craftsmanship. Mindo: Discover the lush cloud forests, perfect for birdwatching and eco-adventures. Quilotoa: Marvel at the stunning turquoise crater lake, ideal for hiking and photography. Baños: Enjoy the natural hot springs and thrilling outdoor activities. Riobamba: Gateway to the magnificent Chimborazo volcano, offering breathtaking views and adventures. Devil’s Nose Train: Experience one of the world’s most thrilling train rides, winding through dramatic Andean landscapes. Ingapirca: Explore the largest Inca ruins in Ecuador, rich in history and archaeological significance. Cuenca: Wander through this charming colonial city, known for its beautiful architecture and vibrant arts scene.
